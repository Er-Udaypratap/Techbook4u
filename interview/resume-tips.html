📄 Resume Tips That Get You Shortlisted

Your resume has seconds to make an impression. Here's how to make every line count.

HR Interview Technical Interview Resume Tips Communication Skills Interview Questions

What Recruiters Scan For First

Most recruiters spend under 10 seconds on an initial resume scan. They're looking for relevant skills, clear structure, and quantifiable achievements — not dense paragraphs of text.

Essential Resume Structure

  • Contact information (name, email, phone, LinkedIn/portfolio link)
  • A short professional summary or objective (2–3 lines)
  • Skills section, matched to the job description's keywords
  • Education, with relevant coursework or achievements
  • Projects or work experience with measurable outcomes
  • Certifications, if relevant

Common Resume Mistakes to Avoid

  • Generic objective statements that don't mention the specific role
  • Listing responsibilities instead of achievements ("Built X" vs "Improved load time by 40% by building X")
  • Spelling and grammar errors — always proofread twice
  • Overly long resumes — aim for one page as a fresher, two max with experience
  • Using an unreadable or overly decorative template

Making It ATS-Friendly

Many companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter resumes before a human sees them. To pass these filters:

  • Use standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills)
  • Avoid tables, columns, or images that ATS software can't parse well
  • Include keywords directly from the job description where genuinely applicable
  • Save and submit as a PDF unless a different format is specifically requested

Quick Checklist Before Submitting

  • Does every bullet point show impact, not just a task?
  • Is the formatting consistent (fonts, spacing, bullet styles)?
  • Have you tailored it slightly for this specific role?
  • Has someone else proofread it?
