❓ Common Interview Questions & Sample Answers

A mix of frequently asked questions across rounds, with strategies for answering each one well.

General & Behavioral Questions

Why should we hire you?

Connect your specific skills and experiences directly to the requirements in the job description, using a concrete example that demonstrates the value you'd bring.

Describe a challenge you faced and how you overcame it.

Use the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep it concise and focus most on the action you took and the measurable result.

How do you handle criticism or negative feedback?

Give a real example showing you listened without being defensive, reflected on the feedback, and made a concrete change as a result.

Tell me about a time you worked in a team.

Highlight your specific contribution and how you collaborated — mention a moment of disagreement or coordination that you helped resolve constructively.

Fresher / Entry-Level Specific Questions

You don't have much work experience — why should we consider you?

Emphasize relevant academic projects, internships, or personal projects that demonstrate applied skills, along with your willingness and speed to learn.

What do you know about our company?

Mention their core products or services, something recent in the news about them, and why that specifically appeals to you — this shows genuine research, not a copy-paste answer.

Questions to Ask the Interviewer

Asking thoughtful questions shows genuine interest. Consider asking about:

  • What does success look like in this role after 6 months?
  • What does the team's day-to-day work typically look like?
  • What opportunities are there for learning and growth?
  • What do you personally enjoy most about working here?

Final Tip

There's rarely one "correct" answer — interviewers are evaluating your reasoning, honesty, and communication as much as the content itself. Practice your answers, but keep them natural rather than memorized word-for-word.
