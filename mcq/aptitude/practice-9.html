Question 1 of 10

If a rectangle has length 10cm and width 5cm, what is its area?

Area of rectangle = length × width = 10 × 5 = 50 cm².
Question 2 of 10

What is the perimeter of a square with side length 6cm?

Perimeter of a square = 4 × side = 4 × 6 = 24 cm.
Question 3 of 10

If x + 5 = 12, what is the value of x?

Solving x + 5 = 12 gives x = 12 - 5 = 7.
Question 4 of 10

What is the value of 15% + 10% expressed as a decimal added together?

15% + 10% = 25% = 0.25 as a decimal.
Question 5 of 10

If 3x = 27, what is x?

Dividing both sides by 3: x = 27/3 = 9.
Question 6 of 10

A pie chart shows 40% for category A. If total items are 250, how many belong to category A?

40% of 250 = 0.40 × 250 = 100.
Question 7 of 10

What is the LCM of 4 and 6?

The least common multiple of 4 and 6 is 12.
Question 8 of 10

What is the HCF (GCD) of 12 and 18?

The highest common factor of 12 and 18 is 6.
Question 9 of 10

If a triangle has angles 60°, 60°, and x°, what is x?

Angles in a triangle sum to 180°, so x = 180 - 60 - 60 = 60°.
Question 10 of 10

What is 2³ + 3²?

2³ = 8 and 3² = 9; 8 + 9 = 17.
