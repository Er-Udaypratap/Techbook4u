Question 1 of 10

What is 25% of 200?

25% of 200 = 0.25 × 200 = 50.
Question 2 of 10

If a shirt costs ₹800 and is discounted by 20%, what is the sale price?

20% of 800 = 160; sale price = 800 - 160 = ₹640.
Question 3 of 10

What percentage is 50 out of 200?

(50/200) × 100 = 25%.
Question 4 of 10

A number increased by 10% becomes 220. What is the original number?

Let x be original: x × 1.10 = 220, so x = 220/1.10 = 200.
Question 5 of 10

If 40% of a number is 80, what is the number?

40% of x = 80 → x = 80 / 0.40 = 200.
Question 6 of 10

What is 15% of 400?

15% of 400 = 0.15 × 400 = 60.
Question 7 of 10

A student scored 45 out of 60 marks. What is the percentage?

(45/60) × 100 = 75%.
Question 8 of 10

The price of an item increased from ₹500 to ₹600. What is the percentage increase?

Increase = 100; percentage increase = (100/500) × 100 = 20%.
Question 9 of 10

If 60% of students passed an exam and 200 students took it, how many passed?

60% of 200 = 0.60 × 200 = 120.
Question 10 of 10

A price of ₹1000 is reduced by 30% and then increased by 10%. What is the final price?

1000 - 30% = 700; 700 + 10% of 700 (70) = ₹770.
