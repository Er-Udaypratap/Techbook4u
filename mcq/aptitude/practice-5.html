Question 1 of 10

A shopkeeper buys an item for ₹400 and sells it for ₹500. What is the profit percentage?

Profit = 100; Profit % = (100/400) × 100 = 25%.
Question 2 of 10

An item bought for ₹800 is sold at a loss of 10%. What is the selling price?

Loss = 10% of 800 = 80; selling price = 800 - 80 = ₹720.
Question 3 of 10

If cost price is ₹250 and selling price is ₹300, what is the profit?

Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price = 300 - 250 = ₹50.
Question 4 of 10

A trader marks an item 20% above cost price of ₹500 and gives a 10% discount. What is the selling price?

Marked price = 500 × 1.20 = 600; after 10% discount = 600 × 0.90 = ₹540.
Question 5 of 10

An article sold for ₹460 results in a loss of ₹40. What was the cost price?

Cost Price = Selling Price + Loss = 460 + 40 = ₹500.
Question 6 of 10

If profit is 25% on a cost price of ₹800, what is the selling price?

Profit = 25% of 800 = 200; selling price = 800 + 200 = ₹1000.
Question 7 of 10

A shop sells a product at a 15% loss for ₹1700. What was the cost price?

Let CP = x; x × 0.85 = 1700 → x = 1700 / 0.85 = ₹2000.
Question 8 of 10

What is the profit percentage if an item bought for ₹150 is sold for ₹180?

Profit = 30; Profit % = (30/150) × 100 = 20%.
Question 9 of 10

A dealer allows a discount of 10% and still gains 8%. If the cost price is ₹500, find the marked price.

Selling price = 500 × 1.08 = 540. Marked price × 0.90 = 540 → Marked price = 540/0.90 = ₹600.
Question 10 of 10

If the cost price of 12 items equals the selling price of 10 items, what is the profit percentage?

CP of 12 = SP of 10 → per-item SP/CP ratio = 12/10 = 1.2, meaning 20% profit.
