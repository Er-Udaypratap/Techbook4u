Question 1 of 10

A man's age is 3 times his son's age. If the son is 12, what is the father's age?

Father's age = 3 × son's age = 3 × 12 = 36.
Question 2 of 10

If today is Monday, what day will it be after 15 days?

15 days = 2 weeks + 1 day; Monday + 1 day = Tuesday.
Question 3 of 10

A clock shows 3:15. What is the angle between the hour and minute hands (approx.)?

At 3:15, the hour hand has moved slightly past 3, creating roughly a 7.5° angle from the minute hand.
Question 4 of 10

If a train leaves at 10:00 AM and reaches its destination at 1:30 PM, how long is the journey?

From 10:00 AM to 1:30 PM is 3 hours 30 minutes, i.e. 3.5 hours.
Question 5 of 10

What is the next number in the series: 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, ?

This is a series of prime numbers; the next prime after 23 is 29.
Question 6 of 10

A sum of ₹5000 is to be divided equally among 4 people. How much does each get?

5000 / 4 = ₹1250 per person.
Question 7 of 10

If the probability of an event is 0.25, what is the probability it does NOT occur?

Probability of not occurring = 1 - 0.25 = 0.75.
Question 8 of 10

A rectangular field is 20m long and 10m wide. What is its area in square meters?

Area = length × width = 20 × 10 = 200 m².
Question 9 of 10

If today is the 1st of the month and it's a Sunday, what day is the 15th?

15th - 1st = 14 days = exactly 2 weeks later, so it's also a Sunday.
Question 10 of 10

A box contains 4 red and 6 blue balls. What is the probability of picking a red ball?

Probability = favorable/total = 4/10 = 0.4.
