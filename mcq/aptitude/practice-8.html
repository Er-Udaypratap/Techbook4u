Question 1 of 10

If all Cats are Animals, and all Animals are Living things, then all Cats are:

By transitive logic, since Cats ⊆ Animals ⊆ Living things, Cats are Living things.
Question 2 of 10

Pointing to a photo, Ravi said, 'She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son.' Who is she to Ravi?

Grandfather's only son is Ravi's father; his daughter is Ravi's sister.
Question 3 of 10

Complete the analogy: Book is to Reading as Fork is to:

A book is used for reading; similarly, a fork is used for eating — matching tool-to-function.
Question 4 of 10

If in a code language, CAT is written as DBU, how is DOG written?

Each letter is shifted forward by 1: D→E, O→P, G→H, giving EPH.
Question 5 of 10

Five friends are sitting in a row. A is to the left of B but right of C. D is to the right of B, and E is at one end. Who is in the middle?

Arranging based on the clues (E, C, A, B, D) places A in the middle position.
Question 6 of 10

Find the odd one out: Triangle, Square, Circle, Rectangle

A circle has no straight sides or angles, unlike the other shapes which are all polygons.
Question 7 of 10

If MONDAY is coded as NPOEBZ, how is TUESDAY coded?

Each letter shifts forward by 1 in the alphabet: T→U, U→V, E→F, S→T, D→E, A→B, Y→Z.
Question 8 of 10

A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?

A is B's sibling, and C (B's sibling) is D's father, so A is D's uncle.
Question 9 of 10

Which number should replace the question mark: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

Differences are 4,6,8,10,12 (increasing by 2); 30+12=42.
Question 10 of 10

If 'PENCIL' is coded as 'QFODJM', what pattern is used?

Each letter of PENCIL is shifted forward by one position in the alphabet to get QFODJM.
