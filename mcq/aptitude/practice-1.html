Question 2 of 10

Find the missing number: 3, 6, 12, 24, ?

Question 3 of 10

What is the next term: 1, 4, 9, 16, ?

These are perfect squares (1², 2², 3², 4²...), so the next is 5² = 25.
Question 4 of 10

All numbers except 9 are prime; 9 = 3×3 is not prime.

Question 7 of 10
