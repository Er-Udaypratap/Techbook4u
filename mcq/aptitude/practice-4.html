Question 1 of 10

A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. What is its speed?

Speed = Distance / Time = 120 / 2 = 60 km/h.
Question 2 of 10

If a train travels at 90 km/h, how far does it go in 30 minutes?

30 minutes = 0.5 hours; distance = 90 × 0.5 = 45 km.
Question 3 of 10

A person walks 5 km in 1 hour. How long will it take to walk 15 km?

Time = Distance / Speed = 15 / 5 = 3 hours.
Question 4 of 10

Two trains start from the same point in opposite directions at 40 km/h and 60 km/h. How far apart are they after 2 hours?

Combined speed = 100 km/h; distance after 2 hours = 100 × 2 = 200 km.
Question 5 of 10

A car covers 240 km at 60 km/h. How much time does it take?

Time = Distance / Speed = 240 / 60 = 4 hours.
Question 6 of 10

If speed is doubled, and distance stays the same, what happens to the time taken?

Time is inversely proportional to speed, so doubling speed halves the time.
Question 7 of 10

A cyclist covers 18 km in 45 minutes. What is the speed in km/h?

45 minutes = 0.75 hours; speed = 18 / 0.75 = 24 km/h.
Question 8 of 10

A train 100m long crosses a pole in 10 seconds. What is its speed?

Speed = Distance / Time = 100 / 10 = 10 m/s.
Question 9 of 10

A man travels at 5 km/h and covers a certain distance in 3 hours. At what speed should he travel to cover the same distance in 1 hour?

Distance = 5×3 = 15 km; speed for 1 hour = 15/1 = 15 km/h.
Question 10 of 10

Convert a speed of 36 km/h into m/s.

To convert km/h to m/s, multiply by 5/18: 36 × 5/18 = 10 m/s.
