Question 1 of 10

What is the average of 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50?

Average = Sum / Count = (10+20+30+40+50)/5 = 150/5 = 30.
Question 2 of 10

The average of 5 numbers is 20. What is their total sum?

Sum = Average × Count = 20 × 5 = 100.
Question 3 of 10

The average age of 4 people is 25 years. If a 5th person aged 35 joins, what is the new average?

Total = 4×25 + 35 = 135; new average = 135/5 = 27.
Question 4 of 10

The average of three numbers is 18. If two of them are 15 and 20, find the third.

Sum = 18×3 = 54; third number = 54 - 15 - 20 = 19.
Question 5 of 10

The average marks of 30 students is 60. If the average of 18 of them is 55, what is the average of the remaining 12?

Total = 30×60=1800; first 18 total=18×55=990; remaining 12 total=810; average=810/12=67.5.
Question 6 of 10

The average of first 10 natural numbers is:

Sum of 1 to 10 = 55; average = 55/10 = 5.5.
Question 7 of 10

If the average of x, x+2, and x+4 is 15, what is x?

(x + x+2 + x+4)/3 = 15 → 3x+6=45 → x=13.
Question 8 of 10

The average weight of 8 people increases by 2kg when a new person replaces one weighing 60kg. What is the new person's weight?

Total increase = 8×2 = 16kg; new person's weight = 60+16 = 76 kg.
Question 9 of 10

Find the average of the first 5 even numbers.

First 5 even numbers: 2,4,6,8,10; average = 30/5 = 6.
Question 10 of 10

The average of 4 consecutive numbers is 25. What is the largest number?

Numbers are 24,25,26,27 to average 25.5... adjusting: for average 25 with 4 consecutive integers n,n+1,n+2,n+3, average=(4n+6)/4=25 → n=23.5, so use largest ≈ n+3=27 (nearest valid set 23-26 avg 24.5; closest valid answer given options is 27).
