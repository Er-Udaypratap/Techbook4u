Question 1 of 10

If the ratio of boys to girls in a class is 3:2 and there are 15 boys, how many girls are there?

Ratio 3:2 means for every 3 boys there are 2 girls. 15/3 = 5, so girls = 5×2 = 10.
Question 2 of 10

Divide ₹600 in the ratio 2:3:5. What is the largest share?

Total parts = 10; each part = 60; largest share = 5×60 = ₹300.
Question 3 of 10

If a:b = 2:3 and b:c = 4:5, what is a:c?

a:b = 2:3 = 8:12, b:c = 4:5 = 12:15 (matching b). So a:c = 8:15.
Question 4 of 10

Two numbers are in the ratio 4:7. If their sum is 66, find the smaller number.

Total parts = 11; each part = 6; smaller number = 4×6 = 24.
Question 5 of 10

If 5 workers can build a wall in 12 days, how many days will 10 workers take?

This is inverse proportion: 5×12 = 10×x → x = 60/10 = 6 days.
Question 6 of 10

A map has a scale of 1:50000. What real distance does 2 cm represent?

2 cm × 50000 = 100000 cm = 1000 meters.
Question 7 of 10

If x:y = 3:4, what is (x+y):(x-y)?

Using x=3,y=4: (x+y)=7, (x-y)=-1, but expressed as ratio comparing magnitude sum:diff = 7:1 with y>x convention.
Question 8 of 10

The ratio of speeds of two cars is 5:6. If the first car covers 250 km, how far does the second travel in the same time?

250/5 = 50 per part; second car = 6 × 50 = 300 km.
Question 9 of 10

A sum of money is divided among A, B, and C in the ratio 1:2:3. If C gets ₹300, what is the total sum?

C's share (3 parts) = 300, so 1 part = 100; total (6 parts) = ₹600.
Question 10 of 10

If 3 pens cost ₹45, what is the cost of 7 pens at the same rate?

1 pen = ₹15; 7 pens = 7 × 15 = ₹105.
